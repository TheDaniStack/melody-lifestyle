Job Opportunities at Melody

Explore career opportunities with Melody Lifestyle and Support Services Limited. We welcome individuals who are passionate about providing quality care, dependable assistance, and professional support.

Nanny / Childcare Provider

We welcome caring and responsible individuals who can support children's daily needs, assist with their development, and help create a safe and nurturing environment.

Opportunities may include full-time, part-time, temporary, or placement-based roles, depending on the available position.

Housekeeper / Domestic Worker

We welcome hardworking and dependable individuals who can assist with household cleaning, organisation, laundry, and other domestic responsibilities.

Opportunities may involve full-time, part-time, temporary, or placement-based domestic support roles, depending on available positions.

Personal Assistant

We welcome organised and proactive individuals who can assist with scheduling, errands, administrative tasks, and everyday personal support.

Applicants should demonstrate good communication, reliability, and the ability to handle responsibilities professionally.

Elderly Companion / Support Assistant

We welcome compassionate and patient individuals who can provide companionship and everyday support to older adults while helping them maintain comfort and independence.

Applicants should be responsible, respectful, and attentive to the individual needs of those they support.

Driver / Personal Support Staff

We welcome dependable and responsible individuals who can provide safe, reliable transportation and practical support for clients when required.

Applicants should demonstrate professionalism, punctuality, good communication, and responsible driving habits where applicable.
